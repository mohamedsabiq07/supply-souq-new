PROCURE Souq - BLACK AND WHITE TRANSPARENT LOGOS

BLACK folder: use on white or other light backgrounds.
WHITE folder: use on black or other dark backgrounds.

These are the clean logos with no glow and no background box.
PNG and WebP contain real alpha transparency. SVG and EPS contain
outlined artwork with no painted background.

WHICH FILE TO USE
- SVG: websites, design tools and scaling to any size.
- PNG 8000: high-resolution 8000 x 2400 logo for general use.
- PNG 4096: smaller everyday logo file for documents and uploads.
- WebP: transparent web image.
- EPS: scalable vector for print and production software.
- Icon: compact PS monogram for apps, profile pictures and small spaces.

Keep the logo's proportions when resizing.
Choose the color that contrasts with the background. Black lettering
will disappear on black; white lettering will disappear on white.
For photos or mixed backgrounds, place the logo over an uncluttered
area with enough contrast.

A white transparent logo may look blank in a white image viewer.
It is visible when placed on a dark background.

JPEG is not included in this transparent bundle because it cannot
retain transparency. Normal solid-background JPGs and all other
formats remain in the complete PROCURE-Souq-Logo-Kit.zip.
